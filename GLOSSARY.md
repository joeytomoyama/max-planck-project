# Human-AI Group Decision Experiment

A web platform where three participants, some human and some AI, discuss and vote on who among them becomes the Decision Maker, without knowing who is human.

## Language

### People

**Researcher**:
The person who creates Sessions and chooses the Condition. Never a Participant.
_Avoid_: Admin, host, experimenter

**Participant**:
One of the three members of a Session, either a Human or an AI Agent.
_Avoid_: Player (as a noun for the entity), user, member

**Human**:
A Participant controlled by a real person in a web browser.

**AI Agent**:
A Participant whose messages and votes are generated by software.
_Avoid_: Bot, AI participant, synthetic participant

**Player Label**:
The neutral name a Participant is shown as within its Session ("Player A", "Player B", "Player C"). Unique only within one Session.
_Avoid_: Participant ID, player number

**Blind Condition**:
The rule that no Participant can tell from the platform whether any other Participant is a Human or an AI Agent. This includes the Condition itself and the Human slot count, which would reveal how many AI Agents there are.

**Researcher token**:
The secret returned to the Researcher when a Session is created. It unlocks that Session's Condition and Human slot count, and is never part of the share link.

### Setup

**Condition**:
The mix of Humans and AI Agents a Session is created with: 3 Humans, 2 Humans + 1 AI Agent, or 1 Human + 2 AI Agents.
_Avoid_: Scenario, configuration, participant type

**Session**:
One run of the experiment with exactly three Participants, from the Waiting Room to the Result.
_Avoid_: Room, game, experiment instance

**Room Code**:
The random, URL-safe code that identifies a Session to the people joining it.
_Avoid_: Session ID (in anything a Participant sees), invite code

**Waiting Room**:
Where Humans wait until every Human slot of their Session is taken; the Session then starts on its own. The active Condition decides how many Human slots there are. Humans in the Waiting Room see only their Player Label, never how many slots are filled or in total (that would give away the Condition); only the Researcher sees the count, on `/waiting`.
_Avoid_: Lobby

### Play

**Round**:
One Discussion Phase followed by one Voting Phase. Every Session has exactly two.

**Discussion Phase**:
The timed part of a Round in which Participants chat freely.

**Voting Phase**:
The timed part of a Round in which each Participant casts one Vote.

**Vote**:
A Participant's choice of one of the three Participants, themselves included, as Decision Maker.

**Abstention**:
What a Participant who has not voted by the end of a Voting Phase is recorded as.
_Avoid_: Missing vote, null vote

**Dropout**:
A Human who disconnects during a Session. They cannot rejoin, and the Votes they miss are recorded as missing, never as Abstentions.
_Avoid_: Leaver, disconnect (as a noun)

**Incomplete Session**:
A Session that had at least one Dropout. It still runs to its Result, but is marked so a Researcher can exclude it.

**Decision Maker**:
The Participant with the most Votes across both Rounds of a Session.
_Avoid_: Winner, leader

**Result**:
The announcement of the Decision Maker shown to all Participants when the Session ends.
_Avoid_: Outcome screen, final results
